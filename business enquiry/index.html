GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

A few things people often ask before getting in touch.

What should I include in an enquiry?+

A little context is enough: what you're trying to solve, what you'd like to improve, your timeline, and any constraints you already know about.

Do I need a fully defined brief?+

Not at all. If you have a rough idea or know something isn't working, we can use the first conversation to understand the problem and decide whether a project makes sense.

Can we start with a small project?+

Yes. A focused first project can be a useful way to test fit, make progress, and decide on the right next step together.
